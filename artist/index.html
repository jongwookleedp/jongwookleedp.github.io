Cinematographer · Artist & Documentary

Jongwook Lee Director of Photography

A cinematographer working across film, music video and commercial, with a focus on documentary portraits of artists and their practice. Cinematographer of the Korea Artist Prize (MMCA) artist documentary series In Workplace, directed by Sunhyuk Kim (caska) — portraying the studios and worlds of leading contemporary Korean artists. Selected filmography includes a short film awarded the Cinéfondation Second Prize at the Cannes Film Festival.
